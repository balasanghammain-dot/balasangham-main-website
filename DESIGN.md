# Design System Direction: Balasangham Kannur
Playful Kerala Editorial + Modern Mobile-First Design

## Dial Declaration
Dial: ENERGY 2 / RHYTHM 3 / MOTION 2

## Visual Identity & Soul
- **Identity**: Balasangham Kannur (Democratic children's and youth cultural movement in Kerala, estd. 1938 Kalliasseri).
- **Mood**: Children's cultural magazine meets Kerala youth movement, festival poster vibrancy, editorial publication clarity.
- **Personality**: Playful, youthful, warm, Kerala-rooted, colorful, editorial, modern, human, premium, minimal, mobile-first.
- **Visual Reference**: `balasangham2026.vercel.app` style (bright golden yellow, warm cream, rich red, playful geometric & organic confetti accents, editorial typography).

## Color Palette
Primary World (Yellow, Orange, Cream):
- Primary Yellow: `#FFC928`
- Bright Golden Yellow: `#FFD84D`
- Warm Orange: `#FF9F00`
- Deep Orange: `#F57C00`
- Warm Cream: `#FFF4D6`
- Soft Cream: `#FFF9E8`
- Pure White: `#FFFFFF`

Deliberate Accent World:
- Deep Red: `#D71920` (Primary text highlights, key CTAs, flag stars)
- Ocean Blue: `#168BD4` (Feature banners, media accents)
- Royal Purple: `#7B2CBF` (Program accents, secondary badges)
- Forest Green: `#2E9E5B` (Initiative badges, eco themes)

Grounding Dark World:
- Dark Brown: `#321A12` (Footers, deep contrast text)
- Near-Black: `#181313` (Heading ink, high-contrast dark surfaces)

## Section Rhythm
1. Yellow Hero (`#FFD84D` -> `#FFC928`)
2. Cream About (`#FFF9E8`)
3. Colorful Programs (`#FFFFFF` / `#FFF4D6` with Red, Purple, Blue, Green accents)
4. Yellow / Warm Orange Featured Event (`#FFC928` / `#FF9F00` - 2026 Kannur Conference)
5. Cream Latest Stories (`#FFF9E8`)
6. Blue Media Feature (`#168BD4` sky banner / cream gallery)
7. Warm Yellow / Cream Final CTA (`#FFD84D` / `#FFF4D6`)
8. Dark Brown Footer (`#321A12`)

## Typography & Malayalam Voice
- Display Headings: Large, bold Malayalam typography (`Anek Malayalam` / `Manjari`), uppercase English sub-labels.
- Body Copy: Generous line-height (`1.75`), comfortable reading size, no walls of text.
- Metadata: Crisp monospace tags for dates, issue numbers, sources.

## Imagery Rules
- Conference Poster (2026 Kannur District Conference): Used strictly in the Featured Event and Conference detail views.
- Hero & Programs: Authentic documentary photography and joyous children illustrations (`happy-children-jumping.png`, `children-troupe-singing.png`, `venalthumbikal-children.jpeg`, `children-dancing.jpeg`).
- Never use biometric / facial recognition features (`FindMyPhotos` is forbidden).
