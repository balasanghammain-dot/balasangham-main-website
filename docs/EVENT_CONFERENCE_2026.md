This document defines the exact visual layout, content, and functional specifications for the **Event Page** of the Balasangham Kannur website. The design and copy strictly derive from the official event announcement and poster.

## 2. Visual Aesthetic & Poster Translation Guidelines

The event page must look like a high-fidelity digital extension of the official conference poster:

1. **Color Tone**:
   - **Dominant**: Crisp White background with bold Revolutionary Red (`#D32F2F` / `#C62828`).
   - **Accents**: Deep charcoal (`#1A1A1A`) for high-contrast typography, Warm Ochre/Gold (`#F59E0B`) for highlights.
   - **Symbolic Touches**: Red five-pointed star (`★`), raised fist (`✊🏻`), white flag motif.
2. **Typography**:
   - Malayalam Headline: Heavy bold Malayalam display font (`Gayathri` bold or `Manjari` bold) with generous letter-spacing.
   - English Sub-headers: Clean sans-serif (`Inter`, `Plus Jakarta Sans`, or `Poppins`).
3. **Hero Card / Banner Treatment**:
   - Replicate the poster's exact visual composition:
     - Prominent theme tag: `[ പോരാട്ടത്തിന്റെ ബാല്യം ✊🏻 ]` in a high-contrast badge or pill.
     - Central bold title: `ബാലസംഘം കണ്ണൂർ ജില്ലാ സമ്മേളനം` / `Balasangham Kannur District Conference`.
     - Highlight box for date and venue: `2026 ഒക്ടോബർ 10, 11 — കല്ല്യാശ്ശേരി`.
     - Official signature: `> ബാലസംഘം കണ്ണൂർ`.
