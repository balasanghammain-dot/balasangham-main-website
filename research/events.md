# Research Document: Balasangham Events & Conferences

## 1. Recurring Annual Observances & Festivals

### Event 1: Bala Kalolsavam (ബാല കലോത്സവം — Children's Arts Festival)
- **Scope**: Grassroots to Statewide (Unit → Panchayat/Area → District → State).
- **Timing**: Annual (typically October – December).
- **Core Principle**: Non-commercial creative expression and democratic participation. Unlike commercial school festivals, Balasangham prioritizes inclusion, cooperative art, and mutual encouragement over fierce rivalry and expensive costuming.

### Event 2: Foundation Day & Flag Day (സ്ഥാപക ദിനവും പതാക ദിനവും)
- **Date**: December 28 (commemorating December 28, 1938 foundation in Kalliasseri).
- **Activities**: Morning flag hoisting with the official flag song (*"ഉണരുക ഉയരുക ശുഭ്രപതാകേ"*) across 20,000+ units, child rallies, founder commemorative sessions, and sweet distributions.

### Event 3: Children's Rights Day (ശിശുദിനം)
- **Date**: November 14.
- **Activities**: Mass children's marches (*കുട്ടികളുടെ റാലി*), cultural festivals, child-rights declaration assemblies, and symposiums on equitable public education.

### Event 4: Anti-War & Peace Days (ഹിരോഷിമ - നാഗസാക്കി സമാധാന ദിനങ്ങൾ)
- **Dates**: August 6 & 9.
- **Activities**: Origami paper crane making, peace rallies, release of white doves, anti-nuclear declarations, and anti-war street painting exhibitions.

### Event 5: Reading Day & Reading Week (വായനാദിനവും വായനവാരവും)
- **Date**: June 19 (P.N. Panicker commemoration week).
- **Activities**: Library enrollment drives, book exhibitions, book reviews, writing workshops, and story sessions in rural neighborhood units.

### Event 6: World Environment Day (പരിസ്ഥിതി ദിനം)
- **Date**: June 5.
- **Activities**: Sapling planting drives (*വൃക്ഷത്തൈ നടീൽ*), local pond and stream restoration, plastic cleanup campaigns, and nature club inaugurations.

## 2. Institutional Delegate Conferences
- **Unit Conferences**: Ward-level gatherings where local children review the year's work and elect unit leaders.
- **Area Conferences**: Block-level assemblies formulating local campaigns (e.g., Pinarayi Area Conference, September 6, 2026).
- **District Conferences**: Representative district assemblies held biennially/triennially (e.g., Kannur District Conference: October 10–11, 2026, Kalliasseri).
- **State Conference**: Apex triennial convention setting Kerala-wide policy (most recent documented: 6th State Conference in October 2022, Thrissur).

## 3. Current Highlight: Kannur District Conference 2026
- **Title (Malayalam)**: ബാലസംഘം കണ്ണൂർ ജില്ലാ സമ്മേളനം
- **Title (English)**: Balasangham Kannur District Conference 2026
- **Dates**: October 10 & 11, 2026 (2026 ഒക്ടോബർ 10, 11)
- **Venue**: Kalliasseri, Kannur (കല്ല്യാശ്ശേരി, കണ്ണൂർ)
- **Auditorium**: PCR Bank Auditorium, Kalliasseri (പിസിആർ ബാങ്ക് ഓഡിറ്റോറിയം)
- **Slogan / Theme**: *"പോരാട്ടത്തിന്റെ ബാല്യം ✊🏻"* (Childhood of Struggle)
- **Historical Significance**: The conference marks the historic homecoming to Kalliasseri, where Balasangham was founded 88 years earlier on December 28, 1938.
- **Role within Website**: One featured current event under the Events branch; not the root entity of the site.
