# Organization Website Benchmark Study

## Research Methodology

Studied 8 real organization websites across children's, youth, scouting, community, and nonprofit sectors. Analyzed information architecture, navigation depth, feature sets, and content types. Organizations selected to span: Indian mass organizations, international children's federations, scouting bodies, and child-focused nonprofits.

## Organizations Studied

### 1. Bharat Scouts and Guides (BSG India)
- **URL**: https://www.bsgindia.org
- **Type**: National children's/youth scouting body
- **Country**: India
- **Audience**: Children 3–25, adult volunteers, trainers
- **Navigation Depth**: 3 levels (mega-menu)
- **Key Sections**: Youth Programme, Governance, Financial Management, Communication (Publications/News/Circulars/Gallery), Membership Growth, Adult Resource
- **Homepage**: Image carousel, Vision, About, History, Fundamentals, Notice Board (40+ items), Age-group cards, Adventure, Partners, Child Protection Policy
- **Leadership**: National leaders, Team BSG, Stalwarts page
- **Documents**: Rules & Regulation (Bye Laws, Rule Book), RTI, Tenders, Financial Statements, Circulars

- **Events**: Schedule of Events, BSG Events portal (external)
- **Publications**: e-book, Monthly Magazine, Annual Report
- **Contact**: Contact page, Help Desk (external)
- **Multilingual**: No
- **Search**: No prominent search
- **Footer**: Minimal (copyright only)
- **Notable**: External sub-portals (Shop, Awards, Help Desk, Alumni, Events, Beginners Course, KYT)

## Key Patterns Discovered

### Pattern 1: Identity-First Architecture
Every organization leads with identity — Who We Are, Mission, History. This is the foundation of trust for first-time visitors. Balasangham's 88-year history and distinguished alumni make this particularly important.

### Pattern 2: Programs as Primary Content
Organizations with active programs make them the centerpiece of the website. Balasangham has 7+ verified active programs — this should be prominent.

### Pattern 3: District/Regional Structure Pages
Indian mass organizations (BSG, SFI, DYFI, KSSP) consistently present their geographic structure. Since this is a Kannur district website, the area committee structure is relevant.

### Pattern 4: News/Updates as Living Content
All organizations maintain some form of ongoing content (News, Blog, Stories, Circulars). For Balasangham Kannur, this would be district updates and event reports.

### Pattern 5: Donation Features Are Not Universal
Donation buttons are prominent on nonprofits (BGCA, Bal Raksha Bharat) but absent from mass organizations (SFI, DYFI). Balasangham does not appear to solicit public donations. This should NOT be assumed.

### Pattern 6: Multilingual Support Is Rare But Needed
Only WOSM (4 languages) and KSSP (Malayalam) have multilingual support among studied sites. Balasangham Kannur needs English + Malayalam.

### Pattern 7: External Sub-Portals for Complex Features
BSG India has 7+ external portals (Shop, Events, Alumni, etc.). Balasangham Kannur should NOT replicate this complexity. Keep everything in one site.
