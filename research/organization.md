# Research Document: Balasangham Organization Identity

## 1. Identity & Official Names
- **Official Name (English)**: Balasangham
- **Official Name (Malayalam)**: ബാലസംഘം
- **Kannur District Body**: Balasangham Kannur District Committee (ബാലസംഘം കണ്ണൂർ ജില്ലാ കമ്മിറ്റി)
- **State Body**: Balasangham Kerala State Committee (ബാലസംഘം കേരള സംസ്ഥാന കമ്മിറ്റി)
- **Scope**: Progressive, secular, and democratic children's cultural, literary, and social organization.
- **Target Demographic**: School-age children between ages 5 and 16 across Kerala.

## 2. Scale & Numerical Metrics
- **State Membership**: Over 1,000,000 children (10 to 12 lakh members).
- **Grassroots Units**: Over 20,000 neighborhood and school units across all 14 revenue districts of Kerala.
- **District Committees**: 14 district units.
- **Scope Classification**:
  - Organization-wide: 1M+ members, 20,000+ units across Kerala.
  - Kannur District: 71-member District Committee (including 17-member District Executive), with 54 elected delegates to the State Conference.

## 3. Mottos and Guiding Principles
- **Philosophical Motto**: *"പഠനം, മനനം, ചലനം"* (Study, Contemplate, Act / Move Forward)
- **Action Slogan**: *"പഠിക്കുക, പോരാടുക, വളരുക"* (Study, Struggle, Grow)
- **Children's Movement Anthem Slogan**: 
  *"പഠിച്ചു ഞങ്ങൾ നല്ലവരാകും, ജയിച്ചു ഞങ്ങൾ മുന്നേറും
  പടുത്തുയർത്തും ഭാരതമണ്ണിൽ സമത്വ സുന്ദര നവലോകം"*
  (Translates: *Through study we shall become noble, through victory we shall advance, building upon Indian soil a new world of beautiful equality.*)

## 4. Official Emblems & Symbols
- **The Flag**: Pure white rectangular cloth banner centered with a five-pointed blood-red star (*രക്തതാര*).
  - *White Banner*: Represents the peace, purity, honesty, and universal fraternity of childhood.
  - *Red Five-Pointed Star*: Represents progressive vision, human dignity, courage, and collective struggle against injustice.
- **The White Dove (*വെള്ളിപ്രാവ്*)**: Symbolizes global peace, anti-imperialist brotherhood, and anti-war solidarity.
- **The Flag Song (*പതാകഗാനം*)**:
  Written for flag hoisting and assembly beginnings across all Balasangham units.
  Preserved verbatim as official user-provided source material.
