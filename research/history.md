# Research Document: Balasangham History & Milestones

## 1. Genesis (December 28, 1938 — Kalliasseri, Kannur)
- **Date**: December 28, 1938.
- **Location**: Kalliasseri, Chirakkal Taluk, Malabar District (present-day Kannur District, Kerala).
- **Original Name**: *Desheeya Balasangham* (ദേശീയ ബാലസംഘം).
- **First Elected Leaders**:
  - President: E.K. Nayanar (then a student activist, later three-time Chief Minister of Kerala).
  - Secretary: Berlin Kunhananthan Nair.
- **Historical Context**:
  - Emerged during the nationalist freedom struggle and agrarian peasant movements in North Malabar.
  - Formed under the political and cultural guidance of leaders including:
    - P. Krishna Pillai (Organizer of Malabar peasant and youth struggles)
    - A.K. Gopalan (AKG) (Leader of peasant hunger marches and working-class resistance)
    - E.M.S. Namboodiripad (Theoretician of democratic pedagogy and cultural awakening)
    - K.P.R. Gopalan (Peasant leader associated with the Morazha struggle)
  - Purpose: To organize peasant and working-class children against feudal bonded labor, child servitude, illiteracy, and caste segregation.

## 2. Anti-Colonial Role & The Kayyur Martyrdom
- Balasangham members actively participated in anti-imperialist processions, distribution of nationalist pamphlets, and peasant support squads.
- **Kayyur Martyr Chirukandan**: Peasant struggle martyr Chirukandan (hanged in Cannanore Central Jail in 1943 during the Kayyur case) was an active Balasangham participant.
- **Choorukkadan Krishnan Nair**: A Balasangham unit secretary in North Malabar, he was convicted in the colonial agrarian trials; because he was a juvenile/minor, his death sentence was commuted to life imprisonment.

## 3. Subsequent Reorganizations
- **1972 Reorganization**: Re-established as *Deshabhimani Balasangham* (ദേശാഭിമാനി ബാലസംഘം) across several districts of Kerala to revive children's literature, art, and reading rooms.
- **December 28, 1980 Statewide Reconstitution**: Formally unified and reconstituted as a statewide democratic federation with its modern child-led constitution, official white flag with blood-red five-pointed star, and the motto *"പഠനം, മനനം, ചലനം"*.
- **1990 Inception of Venalthumbikal**: Evolution of regional vacation theater squads (*Kalivandi*) into the unified statewide *Venalthumbikal* traveling cultural theater troupe.

## 4. Documented State Conferences
- **1st State Conference**: Kottayam.
- **2nd State Conference**: Pilicode, Kasaragod.
- **3rd State Conference**: October 2014, Palakkad.
- **4th State Conference**: December 2016, Perinthalmanna, Malappuram.
- **5th State Conference**: Adoor, Pathanamthitta.
- **6th State Conference**: October 2022, Thrissur.
