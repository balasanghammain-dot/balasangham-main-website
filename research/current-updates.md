# Research Document: Current 2026 Activity & Recent Updates

## 1. Timeline of Recent & Current Organizational Activities (2024–2026)

### Update 1: Balasangham Kannur District Conference 2026 (Upcoming October 10–11, 2026)
- **Date**: October 10 & 11, 2026.
- **Location**: Kalliasseri, Kannur, Kerala.
- **Venue**: PCR Bank Auditorium, Kalliasseri.
- **Theme / Slogan**: *"പോരാട്ടത്തിന്റെ ബാല്യം ✊🏻"* (Childhood of Struggle).
- **Organizational Level**: District (Kannur).
- **Description**: Historic homecoming conference marking 88 years of Balasangham at its 1938 birthplace. Convening elected child delegates from all Area Committees of Kannur to deliberate on child rights, school environments, and elect the new District Committee.
- **Source**: Official announcement poster / Instagram announcement / District Committee notice.
- **Confidence**: HIGH.

### Update 2: Statewide 'Sodarathwena' Secular Children's Collectives (September 21–22, 2026)
- **Date**: September 21–22, 2026.
- **Location**: All Area Committee centers across Kerala, including Kannur.
- **Organizational Level**: Statewide / Area.
- **Description**: In commemoration of Sree Narayana Guru Samadhi Day, Balasangham organized secular children's gatherings entitled *"Sodarathwena"* (സഹോദരഭാവത്തോടെ / In fraternity), emphasizing communal harmony, peaceful coexistence, and resistance against sectarian hatred through cultural games and collaborative art.
- **Source**: *Deshabhimani* ("മതനിരപേക്ഷ കൂട്ടായ്മകളുമായി ബാലസംഘം", Published Sep 22, 2026).
- **Confidence**: HIGH.

### Update 4: Kannur District Conference 2024 (October 5–6, 2024)
- **Date**: October 5–6, 2024.
- **Location**: Pilathara (Kulappuram EMS Vayanashala Auditorium), Kannur.
- **Organizational Level**: District (Kannur).
- **Description**: The 2024 delegate assembly demanded child-friendly environments in all spheres of public life, called for an end to violence against children in Gaza/Palestine, and sought national disaster status for the Wayanad landslides. Elected K. Surya as District President and M.P. Gokul as District Secretary.
- **Source**: *Deshabhimani* (Published Oct 7, 2024).
- **Confidence**: HIGH.
