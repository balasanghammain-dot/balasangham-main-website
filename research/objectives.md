# Research Document: Balasangham Objectives, Ideology & Values

## 1. Stated Purpose & Core Ethos
Balasangham defines itself as a parallel educational, cultural, and social organization for children (*സമാന്തര വിദ്യാഭ്യാസ സാംസ്കാരിക സംഘടന*). Rather than functioning as a conventional youth group managed by adult authorities, it operates as an autonomous child-led democratic platform designed to nurture progressive values, creative talents, and civic responsibility outside the rigid boundaries of formal examinations.

## 2. Core Pillars of Objective

### A. Democratic Agency & Self-Governance (*കുട്ടികളുടെ ജനാധിപത്യം*)
- Children elect their own leadership (Unit President, Secretary, Committee members) through direct voting and consensus.
- Children formulate demands, lead discussions, and organize campaigns.
- Adult advisors (*രക്ഷാധികാരി സമിതി / Conveners*) serve exclusively in supportive, mentor-like capacities without overriding child decisions.

### B. Secularism & Universal Human Fraternity (*മതനിരപേക്ഷതയും മാനവസൗഹൃദവും*)
- Bringing together children irrespective of religious faith, caste origins, gender, economic status, or linguistic backgrounds.
- Actively opposing sectarianism, communal polarization, and exclusionary practices.
- Stated official phrase: *"മാനവസൗഹൃദം, മതനിരപേക്ഷത, ശാസ്ത്രചിന്ത"* (Human fraternity, Secularism, Scientific thought).

### C. Scientific Temper & Rational Inquiry (*ശാസ്ത്രബോധവും യുക്തിചിന്തയും*)
- Cultivating scientific curiosity through astronomy camps (*Shasthra Deepthi*), environmental observation, nature trails, and science workshops.
- Dismantling superstition, blind faith, and pseudo-scientific dogmas through experimental demonstrations and inquiry-based pedagogy.

### D. Child Rights & Social Protection (*കുട്ടികളുടെ അവകാശ സംരക്ഷണം*)
- Active community vigilance against child labor, domestic abuse, sexual exploitation, and severe academic distress.
- Campaigns against narcotic substance abuse and digital exploitation targeting school children (e.g., massive human chains and community pledges).
- Advocacy for equitable, high-quality public education for all children.

### E. Creative, Non-Commercial Arts & Culture (*സർഗ്ഗാത്മക സാംസ്കാരിക വികാസം*)
- Developing children's artistic and literary capabilities through street theater (*Venalthumbikal*), vacation camps (*Venal Kalari*), and art festivals (*Bala Kalolsavam*).
- Prioritizing collective participation and creative joy over commercial competition and trophy commodification.

## 3. Official Slogans and Formulations
- *"പഠനം, മനനം, ചലനം"* (Study, Contemplate, Act)
- *"പഠിക്കുക, പോരാടുക, വളരുക"* (Study, Struggle, Grow)
- *"കുട്ടികൾക്കായി ഒരു നല്ല നാളെ"* (A better tomorrow for children)
- Flag Song Pledge: Resisting hunger, prison walls, unemployment, and oppression standing in the way of Indian childhood's liberation.
