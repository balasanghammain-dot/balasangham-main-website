# Research Document: Balasangham Programs & Activities

## 1. Verified Ongoing Programs Catalog

### Program 1: Venalthumbikal (വേനൽത്തുമ്പികൾ — Summer Dragonflies)
- **What It Is**: Kerala's largest children's traveling cultural and street theater troupe. Recognized as Asia's premier traveling children's theater.
- **Origins**: Started regionally in Kannur and Malabar under the name *Kalivandi* (കളിവണ്ടി), unified statewide as *Venalthumbikal* in 1990.
- **Operating Schedule**: Annually during the summer school vacation (April – May).
- **Format**: Troupe of trained child performers tours hundreds of local centers across assembly constituencies, performing street plays, rhythm recitations, songs, and puppetry.
- **Themes**: Child rights defense, environmental preservation, anti-drug awareness, secular friendship, and scientific inquiry.
- **Source**: ML Wikipedia / The Hindu (2019) / Deshabhimani archives. Confidence: HIGH.

### Program 2: Venal Kalari (വേനൽ കളരി — Summer Creative Camps)
- **What It Is**: Intensive residential and non-residential vacation workshops organized at the unit and area levels.
- **Activities**: Dramatic training, clay modeling, folk singing, indigenous arts, creative writing, public speaking, and child rights education.
- **Operating Schedule**: Summer vacation (April – May).
- **Source**: Organization KB / Public announcements. Confidence: HIGH.

### Program 3: Kilikkoodu Children's Magazine (കിളിക്കൂട് മാസിക)
- **What It Is**: The official monthly print children's magazine published by Balasangham.
- **Content**: Original poems, short stories, folk tales, science features, world affairs, environmental reports, and artwork created and submitted by child members.
- **Circulation**: Circulated widely through primary, upper-primary, and high school units and local libraries across Kerala.
- **Source**: Organization KB / Wikipedia. Confidence: HIGH.

### Program 4: Shasthra Deepthi & Nature Camps (ശാസ്ത്ര ദീപ്തിയും പരിസ്ഥിതി ക്യാമ്പുകളും)
- **What It Is**: Science popularization and environmental protection camps.
- **Activities**: Night sky-watching with telescopes, hands-on scientific experiments debunking superstitious claims/miracles, biodiversity mapping, seed-ball making, and local waterbody cleaning drives.
- **Source**: Deshabhimani / Organization KB. Confidence: HIGH.

### Program 5: Kutti Koottams & Reading Circles (കുട്ടിക്കൂട്ടങ്ങൾ & വായനക്കൂട്ടങ്ങൾ)
- **What It Is**: Grassroots neighborhood gatherings held weekly or fortnightly at local libraries or community centers.
- **Activities**: Book reviews, group reading, film screenings, storytelling sessions, and democratic peer discussions.
- **Source**: Organization KB. Confidence: HIGH.

### Program 6: Anti-Substance Abuse & Digital Safety Campaigns (ലഹരിവിരുദ്ധ ജാഗ്രത)
- **What It Is**: Mass student campaigns mobilizing children against drug trafficking, substance abuse, and predatory digital hazards.
- **Tactics**: Massive human chains (*മനുഷ്യച്ചങ്ങലകൾ*), signature campaigns, flash mobs, street marches, and parent-child community assemblies.
- **Source**: Deshabhimani news reports. Confidence: HIGH.

### Program 7: Sodarathwena Secular Children's Gatherings (സോദരത്വേന)
- **What It Is**: Statewide secular gathering organized at all Area Committee centers to foster brotherhood and reject communal polarization.
- **Occurrence**: Documented on September 22, 2026 across all Area centers in Kerala on Sree Narayana Guru Samadhi Day.
- **Source**: Deshabhimani (Published Sep 22, 2026). Confidence: HIGH.
