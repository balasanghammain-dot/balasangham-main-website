# Research Document: Balasangham Kannur District Unit

## 1. Overview & Organizational Role
Kannur holds a distinctive place in Balasangham's identity as its birthplace and historical heartland. The **Balasangham Kannur District Committee (ബാലസംഘം കണ്ണൂർ ജില്ലാ കമ്മിറ്റി)** coordinates thousands of neighborhood units across 18+ area committees throughout Kannur district.

## 2. Verified Kannur Leadership & Structure
From the verified reporting of the Kannur District Conference held in Pilathara (Kulappuram EMS Vayanashala Auditorium, October 5–6, 2024, published in *Deshabhimani* on Oct 7, 2024):
- **District President**: K. Surya (കെ. സൂര്യ)
- **District Secretary**: M.P. Gokul (എം. പി. ഗോകുൽ)
- **District Convener**: P. Sumeshan (പി. സുമേശൻ)
- **District Coordinator**: Vishnu Jayan (വിഷ്ണുജയൻ)
- **Vice Presidents**: Darshana Sanoj, Amal Prem (ദർശന സനോജ്, അമൽ പ്രേം)
- **Joint Secretaries**: K.V. Aadith, Devika S. Dev (കെ. വി. ആദിത്ത്, ദേവിക എസ്. ദേവ്)
- **Joint Conveners**: T. Satheesh Kumar, P.K. Sheela (ടി. സതീഷ് കുമാർ, പി. കെ. ഷീല)
- **Committee Composition**: 71-member District Committee, including a 17-member District Executive.
- **Outgoing Secretary (2024)**: Anuvind Aithara (അനുവിന്ദ് ആയിത്തര).

## 3. Notable Figures from Kannur
- **E.K. Nayanar**: First President of Desheeya Balasangham (1938, Kalliasseri); later Chief Minister of Kerala.
- **M. Vijin**: Former Kannur district leader and Balasangham State Secretary (2011); currently Member of Legislative Assembly (MLA) for Kalliasseri Assembly Constituency in Kannur.
- **Dr. V. Sivadasan**: Member of Parliament (Rajya Sabha) from Kannur; former Balasangham activist.

## 4. Key Area Committees in Kannur District
Kannur district comprises multiple block/panchayat area committees including:
- Kalliasseri Area
- Pinarayi Area (Area Conference: September 6, 2026, Kottayam Angadi)
- Payyanur Area
- Madayi Area
- Taliparamba Area
- Thalassery Area
- Kannur Town Area
- Mattannur Area
- Iritty Area
- Edakkad Area
