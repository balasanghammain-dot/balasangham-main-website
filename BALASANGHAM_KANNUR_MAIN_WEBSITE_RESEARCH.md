## 1. Executive Summary

Balasangham (ബാലസംഘം) is the largest democratic, secular, and progressive children's cultural and social movement in Kerala and Asia, encompassing over 1,000,000 active child members across 20,000 neighborhood and school units in all 14 revenue districts. Founded on December 28, 1938 in Kalliasseri, Kannur during the historic anti-colonial and peasant uprisings of North Malabar, Balasangham operates on the constitutional principle that children should lead their own democratic forums, electing student presidents and secretaries while adult cultural mentors (*രക്ഷാധികാരി സമിതി*) act strictly as facilitators.

This research report establishes the verified evidentiary foundation for the **Main Balasangham Kannur Website**. The website hierarchy is architecturally established as:
```text
                    BALASANGHAM (Statewide Children's Federation)
                                        ↓
               BALASANGHAM KANNUR (District Committee & Area Units)
                                        ↓
                 SIGNATURE PROGRAMS & CURRENT ACTIVITIES (2024–2026)
                                        ↓
                  RECURRING FESTIVALS & INSTITUTIONAL EVENTS
                                        ↓
             FEATURED EVENT: 2026 KANNUR DISTRICT CONFERENCE (KALLIASSERI)
```
The website is emphatically **not** an event landing page; the 2026 Kannur District Conference (October 10–11, 2026, Kalliasseri) is one current event featured inside the main organization portal.

## 4. Kalliasseri Connection

Kalliasseri holds sacred historical significance for Balasangham:
1. **The Cradle of 1938:** It was in Kalliasseri on December 28, 1938 that the first unit of *Desheeya Balasangham* was constituted under the guidance of peasant leaders resisting feudal land oppression and child servitude.
2. **First Leadership:** E.K. Nayanar, then a young student activist in Kalliasseri/Chirakkal, was chosen as its first president.
3. **Political & Civic Continuity:** M. Vijin, who began his public life as a child in Balasangham's Edat/Madayi units in Kannur and rose to become Balasangham State Secretary in 2011, is currently the Member of the Legislative Assembly (MLA) representing the Kalliasseri Assembly Constituency.
4. **The 2026 Homecoming:** The 2026 Kannur District Conference represents an emotional and ideological homecoming to Kalliasseri, commemorating 88 unbroken years of democratic childhood resistance.

## 6. Objectives, Values & Stated Ideas

Preferring the organization's own documented terminology over external characterization:
1. **Childhood Democracy (*കുട്ടികളുടെ ജനാധിപത്യം*):** Realizing an autonomous space where children deliberate, pass resolutions, and run their own committees, rather than functioning as passive recipients of adult instruction.
2. **Secular Brotherhood (*മതനിരപേക്ഷതയും മാനവസൗഹൃദവും*):** Bridging religious, caste, linguistic, and economic divides among children. Stated motto: *"മാനവസൗഹൃദം, മതനിരപേക്ഷത, ശാസ്ത്രചിന്ത"*.
3. **Scientific Temper & Rationality (*ശാസ്ത്രബോധം*):** Fostering empirical inquiry, sky observation, and scientific demonstrations to eradicate superstitious fear and dogma.
4. **Child Rights Defense (*കുട്ടികളുടെ അവകാശ സംരക്ഷണം*):** Organizing community vigilance against child abuse, child labor, drug trafficking, and hyper-competitive academic oppression.
5. **Non-Commercial Cultural Joy (*സർഗ്ഗാത്മകത*):** Providing artistic platforms (*Venalthumbikal*, *Bala Kalolsavam*) rooted in cooperation, social awareness, and mutual encouragement rather than commercial trophy rivalry.

## 7. Programs and Activities

1. **Venalthumbikal (വേനൽത്തുമ്പികൾ — Summer Dragonflies):**
   - *Nature:* Traveling children's street theater troupe.
   - *Operating Window:* Summer vacations (April–May) annually since 1990.
   - *Scale:* Tens of thousands of performances in rural and urban neighborhood junction centers across Kerala.
   - *Artistic Form:* Original street plays, choral folk music, poetry recitations, and puppet theater addressing child rights, ecology, and constitutional values.
2. **Venal Kalari (വേനൽ കളരി — Creative Vacation Camps):**
   - *Nature:* Intensive residential and day training workshops.
   - *Curriculum:* Theater improvisation, clay sculpture, folk arts, drawing, public speaking, science toys, and indigenous sports.
3. **Kilikkoodu Children's Magazine (കിളിക്കൂട് മാസിക):**
   - *Nature:* Official monthly printed children's periodical.
   - *Content:* Children's stories, poems, science columns, world affairs, and paintings submitted by child members.
4. **Shasthra Deepthi & Nature Camps (ശാസ്ത്ര ദീപ്തി):**
   - *Nature:* Environmental defense and science inquiry camps.
   - *Activities:* Night sky observation with telescopes, scientific debunking of pseudo-science/miracles, biodiversity mapping, seed-ball making, and local waterbody cleanups.
5. **Kutti Koottams & Reading Clubs (കുട്ടിക്കൂട്ടങ്ങൾ):**
   - *Nature:* Grassroots neighborhood peer circles meeting weekly in libraries to discuss books, screen films, and organize local cultural initiatives.
6. **Anti-Substance & Child Safety Vigilance (ലഹരിവിരുദ്ധ ജാഗ്രത):**
   - *Nature:* Mass student-led campaigns against narcotics and digital exploitation via human chains (*മനുഷ്യച്ചങ്ങലകൾ*), rallies, and community pledges.

## 8. Organizational Structure

Balasangham operates through an inverted, child-led pyramid:
```text
                     STATE COMMITTEE (സംസ്ഥാന സമിതി)
             Apex policy, state conferences, statewide campaigns
                                     ↑
                   DISTRICT COMMITTEES (ജില്ലാ സമിതികൾ)
           14 revenue districts (e.g., Kannur 71-member committee)
                                     ↑
                     AREA COMMITTEES (ഏരിയ സമിതികൾ)
         Block/Taluk/Panchayat cluster coordination (18+ in Kannur)
                                     ↑
                    UNIT COMMITTEES (യൂണിറ്റ് സമിതികൾ)
     20,000+ basic grassroots cells in neighborhoods, schools & libraries
```
- Every committee is led by an elected child President and child Secretary.
- Adult cultural mentors serve as Conveners and Patrons (*രക്ഷാധികാരി സമിതി*).
