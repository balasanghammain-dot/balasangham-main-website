## 2. ABOUT

### Who We Are
Balasangham (ബാലസംഘം) is a secular, progressive, and democratic children's movement uniting more than one million school-age children (ages 6 to 18) across 20,000 neighborhood and school units in Kerala. Operating as an autonomous parallel educational and cultural platform, Balasangham enables children to direct their own activities, debate social realities, and practice democratic leadership outside conventional examination pressures.

### Core Ideological Tenets
1. **Childhood Democracy:** Run directly by children. Student delegates elect unit, area, district, and state leaders, while adult cultural mentors act strictly as supportive conveners (*രക്ഷാധികാരി സമിതി*).
2. **Secular Human Brotherhood:** Bringing together children across religion, caste, gender, and economic divides under the banner of mutual solidarity.
3. **Scientific Inquiry & Rationality:** Promoting hands-on science, astronomy, and environmental guardianship to overcome superstition, dogma, and irrational fear.
4. **Defense of Child Rights:** Vigilant community action defending children from child labor, physical and emotional abuse, narcotics, and academic exploitation.

### Tiered Governance Model
- **Unit Committee (Ward / School Level):** The fundamental grassroots cell where every child has an equal vote and voice in neighborhood projects.
- **Area Committee (Panchayat / Municipality Level):** Coordinates neighborhood clusters, vacation camps, and regional cultural troupes across local self-government areas.
- **District Committee (14 Revenue Districts):** Leads large-scale arts festivals, delegate assemblies, and district campaigns (e.g., Balasangham Kannur District Committee).
- **State Committee (Apex Council):** Sets organizational policy, oversees statewide programs, coordinates publications, and convenes triennial delegate conferences.

## 3. HISTORY

### The Cradle of Kalliasseri (December 28, 1938)
Balasangham was born on December 28, 1938 in Kalliasseri, Chirakkal Taluk, Malabar (present-day Kannur District, Kerala). In the midst of agrarian uprisings against feudal landlord exploitation and colonial British rule, peasant children organized under the guidance of pioneering national leaders:
- **P. Krishna Pillai:** Legendary mass organizer who mobilized Malabar youth and peasants.
- **A.K. Gopalan (AKG):** Crusader of the downtrodden and leader of historic peasant hunger marches.
- **E.M.S. Namboodiripad:** Visionary thinker, statesman, and architect of democratic pedagogy.
- **K.P.R. Gopalan:** Peasant leader who defended agrarian workers in Morazha and Kalliasseri.

At its founding meeting, **E.K. Nayanar** (later three-time Chief Minister of Kerala) was elected its first President, and **Berlin Kunhananthan Nair** was elected Secretary.

### Anti-Colonial Resistance & Agrarian Martyrs
Balasangham units formed dynamic communication and support squads during the freedom movement. **Chirukandan**, one of the four immortal Kayyur peasant struggle martyrs executed in 1943, was an active Balasangham participant. Juvenile unit secretary **Choorukkadan Krishnan Nair** was spared capital punishment due to his minority age and served life imprisonment.

### Chronological Milestones
- **1938:** Founding of *Desheeya Balasangham* in Kalliasseri, North Malabar.
- **1940s–1950s:** Mobilization of children's squads in anti-colonial protests, literacy campaigns, and famine relief.
- **1972:** Reorganization as *Deshabhimani Balasangham* across Kerala districts.
- **1980 (Dec 28):** Formal statewide constitutional reconstitution into a unified democratic children's federation.
- **1990:** Establishment of the *Venalthumbikal* traveling children's street theater troupe.
- **2024:** Kannur District Conference at Pilathara, electing the current 71-member District Committee.
- **2026 (Oct 10–11):** District Conference returns to its 1938 birthplace at Kalliasseri.

## 4. OBJECTIVES

1. **Democratic Empowerment:** Provide an egalitarian space where children articulate their opinions, formulate resolutions, and elect student leadership.
2. **Scientific Temper:** Dispel superstition and pseudo-science through empirical demonstration, sky-watching, and inquiry-based learning.
3. **Secular Coexistence:** Uphold the constitutional spirit of fraternity, resisting communal division and prejudice.
4. **Universal Child Welfare:** Safeguard every child's right to play, learn, and grow in a nurturing, exploitation-free environment.
5. **Creative Participation:** Foster cultural, theatrical, and literary skills through collective collaboration rather than commercial trophy rivalry.

## 5. PROGRAMS

### 1. Venalthumbikal (Summer Dragonflies)
Kerala's premier traveling children's street theater. Every summer vacation (April–May), trained troupes of child artists travel through thousands of rural and urban junctions performing street plays, musical choreographies, and puppet theater on child rights and social equality.

### 2. Venal Kalari (Vacation Creative Camps)
Intensive residential and non-residential workshops held across unit and area centers. Children receive hands-on instruction in theater, clay sculpture, folk arts, drawing, public discourse, and indigenous games.

### 3. Kilikkoodu Magazine
The official monthly children's print publication of Balasangham. Packed with original poems, short fiction, science columns, world affairs, and illustrations submitted by child members.

### 4. Shasthra Deepthi & Nature Camps
Science and environmental initiatives featuring telescope-assisted night sky observations, biodiversity documentation, anti-superstition demonstrations, seed-ball scattering, and local waterbody cleanups.

### 5. Kutti Koottams & Reading Clubs
Weekly neighborhood assemblies held in local libraries and community halls where children discuss books, review cinema, and organize community initiatives.

### 6. Anti-Substance & Child Safety Vigilance
Mass community campaigns protecting school students from narcotics, substance abuse, and digital exploitation through human chains, rallies, and student-parent dialogues.
